How to Use the Body Fat % Calculator

The Body Fat Percentage Calculator estimates body composition using the US Navy Circumference Method — a widely validated approach that uses neck, waist, and hip measurements to predict fat percentage. It calculates your fat mass, lean mass, and fitness category.

Enter your height, weight, neck circumference (at the narrowest point), waist circumference (at the navel for men, smallest point for women), and hip circumference (women only, at the widest point). The calculator applies the US Navy formula and shows your body fat percentage and category.

A key nuance: the US Navy method is a measurement-based estimate with an accuracy of ±3–4% compared to DEXA scan (gold standard). For more accurate assessment, DEXA, hydrostatic weighing, or a BodPod provide clinically accurate measurements. The circumference method is best used for tracking changes over time rather than as an absolute figure.

📊 Worked Example

Male, 5'10 (178cm), 185 lbs (84kg), neck 15in (38cm), waist 35in (89cm):

  • Estimated body fat: 19.5%
  • Fat mass: 36.1 lbs (16.4 kg)
  • Lean mass: 148.9 lbs (67.6 kg)
  • Category: Average / Acceptable

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

Healthy ranges: Men: Essential fat 2–5%, Athletes 6–13%, Fitness 14–17%, Acceptable 18–24%, Obese 25%+. Women: Essential fat 10–13%, Athletes 14–20%, Fitness 21–24%, Acceptable 25–31%, Obese 32%+. Women naturally carry more essential fat for hormonal and reproductive functions.

How accurate is the US Navy method?

The US Navy circumference method has been validated with an accuracy of ±3–4% compared to hydrostatic weighing. It's better than BMI (which doesn't distinguish muscle from fat) but less accurate than DEXA. Consistency in measurement technique is crucial — always measure the same spots, at the same time of day, with the same tape tension.

Why does body fat percentage matter more than BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) only uses height and weight — it cannot distinguish between muscle and fat. A heavily muscled athlete may have an 'obese' BMI while having 10% body fat. Body fat percentage directly measures the ratio of fat to lean mass, making it a much more relevant health and fitness metric.

How do I measure my waist correctly?

For men: measure at the navel (belly button) level, standing relaxed (don't suck in!). For women: measure at the narrowest point of the torso. Use a flexible tape measure pulled snug but not tight. Take 3 measurements and average them for consistency. Morning measurements before eating give the most consistent results.

How quickly can I reduce body fat percentage?

Natural fat loss rate is 0.5–1% of body weight per week (in a sustainable deficit). Losing 1% body fat per month is a realistic, sustainable rate. For a 180 lb person, that's about 1.8 lbs of fat per month. Faster rates risk losing significant muscle mass. Track body fat monthly, not weekly, as short-term water fluctuations mask real trends.
